Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026  ·  Version: 1.1.0 (build 4)

Sendora ("the App", "we", "our") is a notification-organizing app that collects the notifications of the messaging apps you choose into one conversation-style inbox on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, what is processed by the third-party advertising service the App uses, and the choices you have. By using Sendora, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information Sendora Accesses on Your Device

With your explicit permission, Sendora uses Android's Notification Access (Notification Listener) to read notifications posted by the messaging apps you enable in Sendora's Settings. This can include the sender's name, the message text, the app the notification came from, the time it arrived, and the sender's picture when the notification includes one. Sendora does not read notifications from apps you have not enabled, and does not access your contacts, photos, files, location, camera or microphone.

2. How Notification Content Is Used

Captured notification content is used only to provide Sendora's core function: showing your notifications in an organized, conversation-based inbox inside the App. Notification content is not used for advertising or ad targeting, is not sent to us, and is not provided to Google, advertisers or any other third party.

3. Local Storage

Notification content captured by Sendora is stored only on your device, in the App's private local database. Sendora does not operate a server or cloud service for your message history and does not upload, sync or back up that content; the App is also excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. Sendora's inbox works without an internet connection.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sendora is free and supported by ads. It shows banner, interstitial, app-open and rewarded ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), a third-party service provided by Google. The SDK, which runs inside the App, may collect and send information to Google, such as: device and app information (for example device model, operating system and app version), your IP address and general location derived from it, your device's advertising ID where available and permitted, and information about ad interactions (such as ad requests, impressions and clicks), along with diagnostic information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve its services. Whether ads are personalized depends on your consent choices, your region and your device's advertising settings.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies, including Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We do not control the data Google collects through its SDK.

5. Consent and Ad Privacy Choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Sendora uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalized. Depending on your region, an "Ad privacy choices" option is available in Sendora's Settings so you can review or change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your device's advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings.

6. Ad-Blocking and Update Checks

Because Sendora is ad-supported, it checks on your device whether ads can be delivered. To do this it reads, locally, your device's Private DNS setting, whether a VPN is active, and whether recent ad requests succeeded, and it performs ordinary DNS lookups of a few Google ad and non-ad web addresses (as any app does when connecting) to see whether ad addresses are being blocked; only a small counter is kept on your device, and this information is not sent to us. In addition, release versions of Sendora download a small configuration file from sendoraapp.tech to check the minimum supported app version. As with any web request, the hosting provider receives standard technical information such as your IP address; no personal data or notification content is sent.

7. Permissions

  • Notification Access: to read notifications from the messaging apps you enable. Without it, Sendora cannot work.
  • Visibility of supported messaging apps: to detect which supported apps are installed so you can choose which ones to monitor.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads and check for required updates.
  • Advertising ID: declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so that ads can be delivered and measured as described above.

8. Your Choices and Control

  • Turn monitoring on or off for each supported app at any time in Settings.
  • Revoke Notification Access at any time in your device's settings; Sendora immediately stops receiving new notifications.
  • Delete conversations in the App; they are removed from Sendora's inbox. To permanently erase all data stored by Sendora, clear the App's storage from your device's App Info screen or uninstall the App.
  • Manage your ad privacy choices in Settings where available, and your advertising ID in your device's settings.

9. Data Security

Your notification content never leaves your device through Sendora, so the main protection is your device itself; we recommend using a screen lock. The App's data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot access.

10. Children

Sendora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Sendora after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Sendora's data practices, contact us at: mohamed.elsamman.dev@gmail.com
